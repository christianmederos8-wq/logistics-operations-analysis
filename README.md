# Logistics Operations & Fleet Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the operations of a logistics and transportation company using a multi-table relational database.

The analysis focuses on understanding load activity, revenue performance, fuel consumption, maintenance costs, delivery reliability, fleet utilization, and safety.

The goal is to transform operational data into actionable business insights that can support decisions related to cost control, operational efficiency, customer performance, and fleet management.

## Business Problem

A logistics company manages thousands of loads, trips, fuel purchases, maintenance events, delivery operations, and safety incidents.

Management needs a clearer understanding of:

- Which customers and routes generate the most revenue.
- How efficiently the fleet is being utilized.
- How fuel and maintenance costs affect operations.
- Where delivery delays and detention occur.
- Which factors may be affecting driver and fleet performance.
- How safety incidents impact operational risk and cost.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze logistics operations and identify opportunities to improve:

- Operational efficiency
- Fleet performance
- Cost control
- Delivery reliability
- Customer and route performance
- Safety performance

## Dataset

The dataset contains multiple related tables representing different areas of logistics operations, including:

- Customers
- Loads
- Trips
- Routes
- Drivers
- Trucks
- Trailers
- Fuel purchases
- Maintenance records
- Delivery events
- Safety incidents
- Facilities
- Driver monthly metrics
- Truck utilization metrics

The core transactional dataset contains approximately **85,000 loads and trips**, covering operations mainly between **2022 and 2024**.
